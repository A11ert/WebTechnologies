About

From nomad camps to palace kitchens

Turkish cuisine started with the food of Central Asian nomads: mutton, yogurt and dried meat that could travel on horseback. Mantı and pastırma come from that time.

Later the palace kitchens of the Ottoman Empire mixed recipes from the Balkans, the Middle East and the Mediterranean. We cook those recipes today, with bread from our own oven every morning.
